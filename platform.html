What each layer does

Software, agents and clinicians, each doing the part it does best. Agents run the workflow; specialists make the clinical decisions.

  1. Capture: WoundRounds and WoundEye

    Nurses document photo, location, measurements and Braden risk at the bedside in WoundRounds, and those assessments go straight to specialist review. At home, patients and carers capture with WoundEye.

  2. Worklist: Wound Solutions AI workflow agents

    For WoundEye, agents check each photo in, file it to the right body site, add it to the certified wound specialist’s worklist, track it until it is reviewed, and schedule follow-ups. They simplify, manage and scale the workflow, and make no clinical decisions.

  3. Clinical Wound Assessments

    Certified wound specialists review WoundRounds assessments and WoundEye submissions, determine type and stage, compute the PUSH score and flag suspected infection. The record shows who decided.

  4. Act and record

    Flags and follow-ups reach the care team dashboard. The confirmed assessment becomes a defensible record, exports to the EHR or plan, and extends the healing trend.
