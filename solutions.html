WoundEye

Setting. Patients at home, family carers, home health and Medicare Advantage care management.

Wound capture and care coordination for individuals and health plan populations.

A patient or carer photographs the wound on whatever mobile device they already own and marks the spot on a body map. WoundAI, our own model, offers a first read. A clinician confirms or corrects it, and that clinician’s assessment becomes the record.
