Compared with Swift Medical

About Swift. A Toronto-based digital wound care company whose Skin & Wound platform has been used for over 50 million assessments.3

Swift is a strong imaging and measurement platform, and its technology also powers PointClickCare’s Skin and Wound app.1 For access to certified wound nurses, Swift customers work with Corstrata, a separate company, through a partnership announced in 2023.2

Where Wound Solutions AI is different

  • One program, one accountable partner. Our certified wound specialists are part of Wound Solutions AI, working in the same record as the software.
  • Built around facility risk. WoundRounds has supported LTACH and skilled nursing wound programs for two decades, with Braden risk, facility-acquired tracking and survey-ready reporting at its core.
  • Families can start the record. WoundEye lets a patient or carer photograph a wound on their own mobile device and send it straight to a specialist’s worklist.

Compared with Net Health

About Net Health. Offers WoundExpert, a wound care EHR, and Tissue Analytics, an AI wound measurement tool.4

Net Health’s WoundExpert is a specialty EHR for wound care management, configurable for single outpatient wound centers or multi-facility organizations.6 Its wound software page describes software, analytics and patient engagement tools; it does not describe clinical review services.4

Where Wound Solutions AI is different

  • Made for the facility, not the clinic. WoundRounds is built for nursing teams in skilled nursing and LTACH settings, where wound-related claims and survey citations concentrate.
  • Specialist oversight included. Our AssureStage Clinical Wound Assessment team reviews documentation, so facilities without a full-time wound nurse still get expert eyes on every wound.
  • Prevention, not only treatment. Risk dashboards and daily review aim to stop facility-acquired wounds before they start.

Compared with PointClickCare Skin and Wound

About PointClickCare. A long-term care EHR vendor. Its Skin and Wound app uses photo recognition and auto-measurement powered by Swift Medical.1

Skin and Wound is a capable documentation module for PointClickCare customers, with timestamped images and audit trails.1 The app is intended to integrate with the PointClickCare EHR; PointClickCare suggests that non-clients look at Swift instead.7

Where Wound Solutions AI is different

  • EHR-independent. WoundRounds works alongside the EHR you already use, so a multi-facility operator can standardize wound care even across different systems.
  • Expert review, not only documentation. Certified wound specialists check the assessments that the bedside team records.
  • Beyond the building. WoundEye extends the same record to patients at home and to health plan care managers.

Questions to ask any wound care vendor

Use these in your next evaluation, including ours.

  1. Who reviews the documentation?

    Is a certified wound specialist checking assessments, and are they employed by the vendor or a separate company?

  2. Who makes the clinical decision?

    Does the record show which clinician confirmed the wound type and stage, and when?

  3. Does it work with our EHR, and every EHR across our facilities?

    Ask for named, live integrations, not plans.

  4. Does it help prevent wounds, or only document them?

    Look for risk dashboards, facility-acquired tracking and follow-up alerts.

  5. What happens after discharge?

    Can patients and families send a wound photo from home, and who sees it?

  6. What results have been published, and where?

    Ask for the setting, the time period and who conducted the study.
