WoundEye, by Wound Solutions AI

A clinician’s eye on the wound, the day someone notices it

Most chronic wounds are first noticed at home, by family. WoundEye turns that moment into an assessment: one photo, two taps on a body map, a few plain questions, and a clinician’s answer the same day. No clinic visit, no special camera, no kit.

How a wound moves through WoundEye

The AI never has the last word. Every record shows which clinician decided, and when.

  1. A guided photograph

    On any mobile device, from the camera or a photo already taken. Location data and camera details are stripped before anything is saved, and the image goes to secure storage, not the camera roll.

  2. Two taps to say where

    First the body region, then the exact spot on a close-up with top and sole views. Every later photo of that spot is filed under the same site.

  3. How it feels, in plain words

    A face for pain, a note in the patient’s own words, and size compared to something familiar: a dime, a quarter, a golf ball. A ruler is welcome but never required.

  4. A first read from WoundAI

    Our model, trained on photographs labelled by wound-care nurses, suggests type, class and stage within seconds, with the line that it comes from a computer model and is not a diagnosis.

  5. A clinician confirms or corrects

    The clinician agrees or changes the result, completes a structured assessment and sets the follow-up. The clinician’s answer is what the patient receives.

Built for the three people around every wound

The same record, seen from three sides. Each person sees what their role needs and nothing more.

The patient

One button

  • Add wound photo, and a list of wounds already sent. Nothing to set up.
  • A first read in seconds and a clinician’s answer the same day.
  • Plain-language care guidance for tonight, with sources, and the emergency signs that mean call 911.
  • Print a report or send a secure link to their own doctor.

The family carer

One sign-in, everyone she cares for

  • Each person is a separate card, so records never mix.
  • She sees only the people linked to her, enforced in the database.
  • Her note and her worry travel with the photo to the clinician.
  • Request a consultation with the patient’s primary care doctor from the record.

The clinician

A caseload that sorts itself

  • One view across organizations and facilities, with live counts by wound type: flagged, follow-up due, recent.
  • Structured assessment with the PUSH Tool 3.0 score computed as you type.
  • Infection screening, treatment, follow-up date and referral in one form.
  • A photo film strip per body site to judge direction at a glance.

A record that holds up

Language we use. WoundAI gives a first suggestion, which a clinician confirms or corrects. WoundEye is clinician-governed decision support.

Allergies sit at the top of every record, so nobody reaches for the wrong dressing or antibiotic. The primary care provider and carer are one tap away. Patient education, AI-assisted or written by the clinician, is kept with the date it was provided.

When a follow-up assessment clears a suspected infection, the flag on the dashboard clears with it. What a clinician records at the bedside is what the next clinician sees the next morning: nothing is re-typed and nothing is remembered.

Privacy

Integrations

WoundEye connects to what you already run: structured export in FHIR R4, JSON and CSV; a pull API with keys; signed webhooks that push new records on a schedule, with a delivery log; and PDF reports ready for the chart. Where a clinic’s scheduling or EHR is connected through FHIR, patients can schedule a visit from their care plan.
