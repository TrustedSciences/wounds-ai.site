Published studies and posters

Newest first. Links open the original publication or poster.

  1. Conference poster2025Symposium on Advanced Wound Care, Fall

    Evaluating the Efficacy of a Hybrid Approach for Wound Management and Risk Prevention in a 236-bed Long Term Acute Hospital

    Michael Seabock, RN, WCC, The Hospital for Special Care; Rhett A. Gustafson, BA, WoundRounds

    Setting
    236-bed LTACH, New Britain, Connecticut
    Design
    Retrospective analysis of 2017–2024 hospital-acquired pressure injury and Braden risk data
    Findings
    Full-thickness HAPI rate down 70.18% against the four-year baseline, and 78.95% in the most recent two years

    Read our summaryPublished abstract (WOUNDS supplement)Full poster

  2. Conference poster2014WOCN Society Annual Conference

    QAPI for Wound Care: A Case Study on Quality Assurance Performance Improvement

    Anne Scheurich, BS, RN, CWOCN, WoundRounds; Michelle Stuercke, BSN, MPA, DNP, NuCare Services

    Setting
    Long-term care facility
    Design
    Performance improvement case study with root cause analysis and corrective actions
    Focus
    Preventing facility-acquired pressure ulcers under the QAPI requirements of the Affordable Care Act

    Poster abstract (WOCN)

  3. Peer-reviewed2012Ostomy Wound Management

    An Observational Study to Assess an Electronic Point-of-Care Wound Documentation and Reporting System Regarding User Satisfaction and Potential for Improved Care

    Kurtz, Florczak, Scheurich, Croghan, Sheridan, McGill, McClain

    Setting
    Provena Heritage Village, a 51-bed skilled nursing facility in Kankakee, Illinois
    Design
    Two-month prospective observational study of 38 residents with pressure ulcers; nine nurses surveyed before and after
    Findings
    Ease of use rose from 3.3 to 4.7 and wound management effectiveness from 3.3 to 4.4 on five-point scales

    Full article

  4. Conference posterSAWC Fall4th Annual Symposium on Advanced Wound Care, Fall

    Reducing and sustaining facility-acquired pressure ulcers: Presence Heritage Village

    Presence Health and WoundRounds

    Setting
    51-bed skilled nursing facility, Presence Health
    Design
    Seven-month before-and-after study
    Findings
    Facility-acquired pressure ulcers down by over 80%, attributed to better documentation and team communication

    Study announcementPoster on woundrounds.com
